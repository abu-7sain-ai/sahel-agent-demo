# Sources for knowledge/services.json

Accepted sources only (constitution section 5): e.gov.kw, official ministry and authority sites, Sahel's official channels, and Al-Qabas, Al-Anba and Al-Rai for announcements.

PACI and MOI service names and categories come from the constitution (Sahel screenshots). Requirements, documents, fees and durations below were researched on 2026-10-26. No screenshots were provided for Justice, Health, Electricity and Water, or Manpower, so their category names in services.json are inferred and every record carries path_verified: false. The agent marks those paths as approximate.

The per-record notes and full URLs are in knowledge/research-draft.json.

All pages were accessed on 2026-10-26. The "page date" is the article or publication date where the page shows one. Long Al-Rai URLs are shortened to their article ID here; the full URLs are in research-draft.json.

## Looked for and could NOT confirm
- **Marriage continuity certificate in Sahel:** I could not confirm the exact in-app title or category. The Sahel service is confirmed as a "شهادة إعلام رسمي (خلو زوجية / استمرارية زواج)", but the category name was never published, so the path is unverified. I also found no Sahel-specific fee or documents.
- **Kuwaiti passport renewal fee in Sahel:** Not confirmed. The e.gov.kw RenewPassport page returns 404, both in Arabic and English. A search snippet showed 3 KD and 6 KD, but I could not verify it, so it is not used.
- **Full MOJ list:** The Al-Qabas article "10 new services" did not load. Its contents are unknown.
- **MOH in Sahel:** I found no medical-report request service and no stand-alone birth-certificate service. The birth certificate appears only inside the "رحلة المولود" journey, and that article gives no requirements or fees.
- **MEW in Sahel:** Paying your own bill, the clearance certificate (براءة ذمة) and meter-reading submission are not confirmed as Sahel services. The sources only show them on the MEW portal. The MEW services list (22 Jan 2022) is old.
- **PAM:** Work-permit issue and transfer services are on the "أسهل" business portal, not Sahel Afrad, so they are excluded. "سهل أعمال" items, such as updating national labour data (https://www.alanba.com.kw/1235196), were not checked and not included.
- **PACI:** I found no page for "دفع الرسوم فرد / أسرة". No page states processing durations.
- **MOI:** Residency renewal fees and non-domestic residency renewal via Sahel were not found. For lost documents, only residents' lost passports are confirmed; I could not confirm other documents or the exact service title. No fees were found for the emergency travel document or the criminal record certificate.
- **Categories:** No source gives the Sahel category name for the Part B services. Those fields are left "" so the categories from the screenshots (constitution section 5) are used.

## Merge decisions
- The in-person e.gov.kw birth-certificate record (0 KD, MOH registry) is not a Sahel service, so it is kept here and not added to services.json. In Sahel, the agent points to رحلة المولود (MOH) and then استكمال بيانات مولود (PACI).
- PACI إصدار شهادة فرد: the 1 KD fee is for the non-Kuwaiti in-person certificate only, and the record says so.
- Requirement lines that only repeated a menu path were removed, because the path comes from name and category.
- MOJ marriage certificate: the 1 KD and 10-minute figures describe the counter procedure, so they are not used for Sahel.
